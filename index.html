Questions

What age is this for?

MyPuppy is built for puppies aged 8 to 16 weeks who have just come home. It still works for older puppies, but some early tasks will feel easy.

What training method does it use?

Reward-based training only: treats, praise and play. No punishment, no shock or prong collars.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. The plan only moves forward when you complete a day, and your coach picks up where you left off.

Do I need to install an app?

No. MyPuppy runs in your phone or computer browser. Sign in and your progress is there.

Does this replace a vet or a trainer?

No. MyPuppy gives general training guidance. For health problems see your vet, and for aggression or severe anxiety see a certified trainer or vet behaviourist. The app tells you when.
